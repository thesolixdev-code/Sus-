How to enable "Join Me" settings in Roblox

Ensure your target account or your own profile allows friends/followers to join games.

Open Roblox Privacy Settings ↗
Step 1: Open Settings

Log in to Roblox and open Gear Icon → Settings → Privacy tab.

Step 2: Who Can Join Me?

Scroll to "Other Settings" and locate Who can join me in experiences?

Step 3: Select Everyone

Set it to Everyone or Friends & Followers to allow session detection.
